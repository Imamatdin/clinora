## Delivered behavior

An explicitly authorized clinical owner or invited clinician can sign in, select
their clinic, search/create permitted fictional patients, create an exact/date-only/
undated encounter and save a four-field unapproved note in PostgreSQL. Reloading or
restarting the compiled server reopens identical saved content and revision from
the patient timeline. Distinct names/records are not merged; optional medical-record
numbers are unique within the clinic. Each visit retains date precision/timezone,
entry time, location, specialty, clinician and its own unapproved assessment.

Registration creates no clinical privilege. Owners are provisioned only by explicit
CLI credentials and clinical permission. Owner/admin role alone cannot read charts.
Invitations are email/clinic scoped, hashed, expiring and single-use. Invited
clinicians have assigned-patient access; creating a chart assigns it to its creator.
Sessions are server persisted, token hashes only, with HttpOnly/SameSite cookies,
deployment-aware Secure, CSRF/Origin checks, throttling and live revocation checks.

Saves append immutable server-controlled document snapshots and atomically update
the head, audit and idempotency result. Two saves from the same base cannot both
succeed. Same-key retries replay their original result, including simultaneous
requests; changed payload conflicts. The browser retains unsaved text on errors and
conflicts, provides explicit latest-version comparison and guards dirty navigation.
No clinical browser storage is used. Missing examination values remain null/blank.

These are implemented behaviors, not mocked APIs or diagram-only contracts. There
is no patient portal, clinical approval/export, audio, AI, bot, live integration,
billing or production deployment. Saving an assessment does not approve a diagnosis
or execute a treatment decision. Professional credentials remain explicitly unverified.
