## Delivered behavior

- Admit an inpatient visit to a free bed with department, attending doctor and expected discharge; room/bed map and room-ordered ward list, with a my-patients filter.
- Transfer atomically with the same closing/opening timestamp; optional caretakers with name, phone and daily service.
- Retry-safe daily bed/caretaker charges on demand and inside business-day opening; historical prices and dated clinic rules; one bed charge per admission/date.
- Add ward services; medicines/stock-tracked goods call task 012 dispensing atomically, reuse its charge and preserve `source=inpatient`. Short stock fails without a charge or movement.
- Discharge settles the stay, stores an immutable invoice, closes the visit and releases the bed. Server-rendered A4 PDF opens directly; cashier sees the same visit balance and can collect afterwards.
- Doctor reads require the attending relationship and a current patient assignment. Ward writes require inpatient/manager roles; current membership is checked on retries too.
- Phone/desktop screen and dictionaries for Uzbek Cyrillic, Uzbek Latin and Russian; inpatient staff land on the ward screen.
- Changing stays clears old invoice/form state; a failed invoice reload shows an error without stale amounts.

## Decisions and changes

- New `018_inpatient_history.sql`; existing migrations are unchanged. Earlier numbers were occupied by other tasks before integration.
- Rule changes take effect tomorrow in the clinic timezone. Prior rule versions and charge prices are immutable.
- Daily runs settle dates through yesterday; today's charge is finalized at discharge. A transfer date uses the first bed occupied that date, priced at that assignment/date's start.
- The minimum-stay shortfall is quantity on the discharge date's single dated line; previous charges are never rewritten. Caretakers follow date-inclusion flags without a patient minimum-stay surcharge.
- Inpatient writers take a clinic lock, then visit, then admission. Transfer timestamps use the clock after waiting, rather than transaction start.
- Shared integration: one business-day hook, route/module/dictionary/style registry entries, pharmacy dispensing source and active multi-day stay support, and visit-first write locks in pharmacy/doctor charge creation.
- The discharge trigger also prevents other modules from inserting charges after discharge. The immutable discharge PDF records the balance then; cashier billing remains the authority for later payments/refunds.
- Owned code: `src/modules/inpatient/**`, `src/server/routes/inpatient.ts`, `src/web/inpatient/**`, `src/web/i18n/inpatient.*`; tests in `tests/integration/inpatient.test.ts`, `tests/browser/inpatient.spec.ts`, `tests/helpers/inpatient.ts`.
