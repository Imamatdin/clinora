## Delivered behavior

- Day close rejects open cash shifts, reconciles records and stores an immutable report, including each shift's expected/counted cash and discrepancy. A4 and 80 mm server PDFs are available.
- CEO live/date-range dashboard: patient cohorts, money by method, refunds, expenses, balances, doctor/department productivity and acquisition sources; totals open their underlying records.
- CEO appends dated doctor split rules; doctors see only their own rule history and earnings. A later service uses its own recorded date's rule, including on an ongoing visit.
- Payroll uses the existing billing library, carries signed corrections, rejects overlapping final periods and stores immutable aggregate/daily snapshots for arbitrary period reads.
- CEO PDF/XLSX exports and audited clinic CSV ZIP; exact integer UZS, formula-safe CSV and no authentication secrets/raw audio in the archive.
- Close queues a patient-free financial notice and attempts delivery through task 004 after commit. Tests use the recording Telegram adapter, not real messages.
- Limestone/aubergine/copper screens, large controls, compact phone filters, explicit loading/error/empty states; Uzbek Cyrillic, Uzbek Latin and Russian report interfaces.
