## Delivered behavior
1. Caretaker fee or transfer bed without a price covering its billed dates is refused (422). Accrual skips an unpriced bed/caretaker day and flags it (`inpatient_attention`, shown on the ward screen and in the discharge invoice) instead of failing day open, close or discharge. A service's first price may be backdated; later prices stay forward-only. Active stays are charged by the next run once priced.
2. Lab confirm writes the technician to `completed_by` only. Doctor orders set performer = ordering doctor; registration lab lines have none. Payroll and the report's per-doctor breakdown include only doctor-role performers. Migration 025 rewrites existing lab lines (lines already in a final payroll period are left as recorded).
3. Doctor shares count only completed lines, dated by completion. `POST charges/:id/cancel` (registrar, cashier, CEO, administrator; a doctor only their own orders) cancels an unpaid ordered line with a reason, voids its line discounts, reallocates receipts across a visit discount, refuses if that would overpay. `GET|POST business-days/:id/no-show` cancels visits with only ordered, unpaid lines (UI: prompt before close).
4. Cash-out takes a per-press key (replay returns one row), needs CEO/administrator approval above `clinic_settings.cash_out_approval_threshold_uzs` (default 0 = always; a manager self-approves), and the shift close report lists every cash-out with its approver.
5. `POST business-days/:id/reopen` (CEO, reason): today's most recent closed day only, no later day, not inside a final payroll period. The snapshot is archived in append-only `business_day_close_versions`; the trigger allows the reopen only via a transaction-local flag after archiving. Re-close writes a new snapshot and re-queues the notice.
6. Day close accrues bed/caretaker days through the closing date before the snapshot.
7. Surgery outside the open business day needs `backdateReason` from a CEO/administrator (doctor: 422 without, 403 with). A new final payroll period must start the day after the latest final one. A cashier's day report totals only their own shifts.
